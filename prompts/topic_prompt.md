# Topic Selection & Angle Refinement Prompt

You are a senior YouTube explainer producer specializing in psychology, behavioral science, cognitive biases, paradoxes, and human behavior.

I want to identify a compelling topic and develop its conceptual storytelling angle for a visually captivating timed explainer video.

---

## Input Variables

- **My rough topic or area of interest is**:
  `"{USER_TOPIC}"`

- **Target Audience**:
  `"{TARGET_AUDIENCE}"` (Default: General audience, particularly US viewers interested in psychology, human behavior, and thought-provoking concepts.)

- **Goal**:
  `"{GOAL}"` (Default: Give viewers a lightbulb "aha" moment by presenting an unexpected aspect of human behavior through an engaging everyday scenario.)

---

## Output Requirements

Please provide:

1. **Topic Name:** The recognized name of the paradox, psychological effect, theory, or phenomenon.

2. **3 Compelling Video Titles / Angles:** Ranked by curiosity and potential viewer engagement.

3. **The Core Concept:** Explain the phenomenon in one sentence.

4. **Opening Scenario:** Describe a believable, everyday fictional situation involving people that gradually demonstrates the phenomenon. Do not reveal the concept immediately.

5. **Story Progression:** Explain how the scenario develops, where the contradiction or realization occurs, and how the concept should be revealed.

6. **Concept Explanation:** Explain the actual psychological phenomenon in simple language.

7. **Origin & Discovery:** Identify its creator, researcher, or historical origin if established.

8. **Supporting Research:** Identify a credible study or experiment, its researchers, methodology, and findings.

9. **Real-World Consequences:** Explain how this phenomenon can negatively influence people's lives and decisions.

10. **Visual Storytelling Potential:** Suggest the characters, objects, environments, and visual contrasts that could communicate the concept through a timed slideshow.

11. **Why This Topic Works:** Briefly explain its curiosity factor, relatability, and potential to keep viewers watching.
