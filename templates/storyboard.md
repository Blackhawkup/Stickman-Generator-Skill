# Storyboard: Chatbot vs AI Agent

**Audience**: Tech-interested audience & everyday users.  
**Goal**: Explain the fundamental distinction between passive LLMs and autonomous agents in 35 seconds.

| # | Trigger Phrase | Slide Subject | Visual Headline | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "car just broke down" | Compact car broken down by roadside | THE BREAKDOWN | Fade in |
| 2 | "ask a chatbot" | Smartphone screen showing bulleted list | CHATBOT ADVICE | Fade |
| 3 | "do all of it" | Overwhelmed person with floating tasks | ALL ON YOU | Fade |
| 4 | "ask an AI agent" | Glowing AI agent node orchestrating | ENTER THE AGENT | Fade |
| 5 | "calls the garage" | Dashboard showing auto-booked tasks & checks | FULLY AUTOMATED | Fade |
| 6 | "just go home" | Relaxed person resting at home with coffee | PEACE OF MIND | Fade |
| 7 | "does it for you" | Bold visual contrast: Passive vs Agent | TELLS VS DOES | Fade / Zoom |
